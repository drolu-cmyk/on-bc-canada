Privacy

Clear information about data use.

The public site is designed to provide useful information without requiring an account or asking visitors for personal information.

Public website

SozoRock Canada does not sell personal information or use this site for behavioural advertising. The public pages are intended to be usable without signing in.

Technical delivery

Cloud and security providers process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the site. This can include an IP address, request time, browser or device information and the pages requested.

Program participation

When personal information is required for a program, the collection purpose is stated where the information is requested. Program records are kept separate from ordinary public browsing and are limited to information needed for participation, support, administration or legal obligations.

External services

External services operate under their own privacy terms. Review those terms before providing personal information to another organization.

Access and questions

Privacy questions and requests relating to personal information can be routed through the Contact page.
