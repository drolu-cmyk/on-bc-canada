Privacy

Clear information about what we collect.

You can browse the public site without creating an account. Personal information is collected when you choose to submit a form or use a protected administrative service.

Public browsing

SozoRock Canada does not sell personal information or use the public site for behavioural advertising. Infrastructure providers process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, request time, device or browser information and the page requested.

Forms

Program interest, partnership, media and general enquiry forms collect the information shown on the form so we can record and respond to the request. Required consent is shown before submission. Do not include sensitive personal information that is not needed for your enquiry.

Where submissions are handled

Website submissions are stored in our AWS Canada environment and are available only through protected administrative access. A notification can also be sent to an authorized SozoRock Canada recipient so a submission is not missed.

Retention

Information is kept only as long as it remains useful for the purpose stated when it was collected, subject to security, operational and legal requirements.

Other services

When you follow a link to another organization, that service’s privacy practices apply. Social sharing, cloud services and other external destinations are outside this policy once you leave the SozoRock Canada site.

Questions

Use the general enquiry form for a privacy question or request concerning information you submitted through this site.
