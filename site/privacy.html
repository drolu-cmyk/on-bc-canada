Privacy policy.

This page explains how the public SozoRock Canada website handles information and how privacy is addressed when someone moves from public information into program participation.

Public website

The public website is designed to be useful without requiring a visitor to create an account or submit personal information. We do not use this site to sell personal information or to serve behavioural advertising.

Technical delivery

Like most websites, infrastructure providers can process limited technical information needed to deliver, secure and operate the site. This can include an IP address, request time, browser or device information, and the pages requested. SozoRock Canada uses cloud infrastructure to make the site available and protect it from misuse.

Forms and program participation

When a page asks for personal information, it states the purpose of the collection where the information is requested. Program records are handled separately from this public website and are limited to information needed for participation, support, administration or legal obligations.

External services

Links can lead to services operated by other organizations. Their privacy practices apply when you use those services. Review the relevant terms before providing personal information to a third party.

Retention and access

Information is retained only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to operational and legal requirements. Requests relating to personal information can be routed through the contact page.

Changes

This policy can be updated when the website, program or applicable requirements change. The current version published here governs the public site.
