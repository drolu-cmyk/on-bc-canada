Access is part of the learning design.

The program is built to support different devices, schedules, bandwidth conditions and ways of showing understanding.

  • ContentClear structure, readable contrast, captions, transcripts and accessible documents where applicable.
  • ParticipationVirtual learning designed for keyboard access, mobile use and lower bandwidth conditions.
  • EvidenceWhere appropriate, learners can demonstrate understanding through more than one accessible format.
  • SupportAccessibility questions can be raised through the published support route.
