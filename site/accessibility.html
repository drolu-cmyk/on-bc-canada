Accessibility

Access is part of the design.

People can reach the information, understand it and participate without unnecessary barriers. The public site and learning experience are designed around that principle.

  • WebsiteResponsive layouts, keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast and semantic structure support navigation across desktop and mobile devices.
  • Learning contentMaterials use clear structure and accessible formats, with captions, transcripts or alternatives where they are relevant to the learning experience.
  • ParticipationVirtual delivery is designed to work across different schedules, devices and connection conditions rather than assuming one ideal setup.
  • Demonstrating learningWhere the learning objective allows it, more than one accessible format can be used to show understanding.
  • SupportIf an access barrier is affecting participation, use the contact page so the issue can be routed appropriately.
