Accessibility

Access is part of the design.

The public site and virtual learning experience are designed to reduce avoidable barriers across devices, connection conditions and ways of navigating content.

  • WebResponsive layouts, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure and readable contrast are built into the public experience.
  • LearningMaterials favour clear structure and accessible formats. Captions, transcripts or alternatives are used when they are relevant to the learning objective.
  • ParticipationVirtual delivery is designed for different devices, schedules and connection conditions rather than assuming one ideal setup.
  • FeedbackIf a barrier prevents you from using the site or participating in a program, use the general enquiry form and describe the barrier.
