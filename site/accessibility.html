Accessibility

Designed for more ways to participate.

SozoRock Canada builds its public site and virtual learning experience for people using different devices, assistive technology, connection speeds and ways of navigating information.

What that means in practice.

Accessibility is treated as part of the experience rather than a separate feature. We use semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast and responsive layouts across the public site.

Learning materials use clear structure and accessible alternatives when the format calls for them.

  • NavigationPages are structured for keyboard use and assistive technology, with meaningful headings, labels and focus states.
  • ReadingType size, contrast, line length and spacing are set for legibility across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
  • Learning materialsCaptions, transcripts and alternate formats are included when they are relevant to the learning activity.
  • Connection and devicesVirtual participation does not depend on one device, one browser size or one ideal connection condition.
