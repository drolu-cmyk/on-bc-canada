Programs

Skills for systems people actually use.

Four connected areas of practice help learners understand technology, manage risk and make decisions that can stand up to scrutiny.

One foundation. Four areas of practice.

The program begins with systems, data, security and technical communication. Learners then apply that foundation to the areas below.

01

Applied AI

Problem framing, data, model behaviour, evaluation and workflow design. The emphasis is on using AI where it adds value and recognizing where human judgement still matters.

02

Cybersecurity GRC

Governance, risk, controls, assurance, evidence and incident readiness. Learners connect policy requirements to the operational proof organizations need to make defensible decisions.

03

AI Governance

Inventory, impact assessment, procurement, oversight, monitoring, change and retirement. Learners examine how organizations govern AI across its full lifecycle.

04

Cloud

Architecture, identity, workload security, resilience and cost. Learners build enough technical depth to understand how modern services are designed and controlled.

Built to outlast a tool.

Platforms will change. The program is designed around concepts, evidence and decision quality so learners can adapt without starting over each time a product changes.
