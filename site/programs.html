Four areas of practice built on the same foundation: systems, data, security, evidence and clear technical communication.

Applied AI

Frame the problem before choosing the model. Work with data, evaluate outputs, understand failure modes and design workflows that keep human judgement where it belongs.

02

Cybersecurity GRC

Move from policy language to evidence. Connect risk, controls, assurance, incidents and reporting to the decisions organizations have to defend.

03

AI Governance

Govern AI across its lifecycle: inventory, impact, procurement, oversight, monitoring, change and retirement.

04

Cloud

Understand architecture, identity, workload security, resilience and cost well enough to ask better questions and make better technical decisions.
