Stories

Work worth showing.

Stories matter when they reveal what changed, what was learned and why the work was useful. We publish them with permission and enough context to be credible.

What belongs here.

We are building a record of learning, projects and partnerships rather than a collection of promotional testimonials. Each story helps a reader understand the challenge, the work and the result.

Learner stories

How people use new capability in their work, studies or community context, including what they found difficult and what they can now explain or build.

Project stories

Applied work that connects technology to a practical problem, with attention to evidence, limitations and responsible use.

Partner stories

What organizations contribute, what they learn and what changes when a partnership creates genuine value.

No invented case studies.

New stories appear only when there is a completed record and permission to publish it. This page defines the standard used for publication.
