Curriculum

Foundations that transfer when tools change.

The learning sequence moves from systems and evidence to AI, security, governance, cloud, and professional practice.

Common Core

Six modules. One transferable foundation.

Each module produces evidence, names its stable concepts, and includes a substitution path for changing tools and platforms.

CC-101

Systems Thinking for Digital Work

Map boundaries, actors, incentives, constraints, decisions, and failure points.

CC-102

Data, Evidence, and Privacy

Trace data lifecycles, provenance, quality, classification, purpose, and retention.

CC-103

Computing, Networks, and Cloud Foundations

Build a working model of computation, networks, identity, services, and cloud boundaries.

CC-104

Security, Risk, and Control Thinking

Connect assets, threats, risk, controls, assurance, and evidence.

CC-105

AI Systems Foundations

Understand models, data, evaluation, failure modes, oversight, and operational boundaries.

CC-106

Professional Practice and Technical Communication

Turn evidence into clear decisions, limitations, handoffs, and accountable defenses.

Specialization domains

Application follows understanding.

Applied AI

Problem framing, data and evidence, architecture, evaluation, oversight, and operations.

Cybersecurity GRC

Risk-to-control-to-evidence practice for governance, assurance, incidents, and metrics.

AI Governance

Lifecycle inventory, impact, procurement, operation, monitoring, change, and retirement.

Cloud

Architecture, identity, workload security, resilience, cost, and assurance.
