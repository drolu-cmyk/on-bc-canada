Curriculum

Start with the system. Then use the tool.

Six common modules establish the foundation before learners move into Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud.

Common technical foundation.

The sequence moves from systems and data to security, AI and professional communication. It gives learners a shared language before they specialise.

  • CC 101Systems Thinking for Digital Work. Map actors, boundaries, incentives, constraints, decisions and failure points.
  • CC 102Data, Evidence and Privacy. Understand data quality, provenance, classification, purpose and retention.
  • CC 103Computing, Networks and Cloud Foundations. Build a working model of computation, networks, identity, services and cloud boundaries.
  • CC 104Security, Risk and Control Thinking. Connect assets, threats, risk, controls, assurance and evidence.
  • CC 105AI Systems Foundations. Understand models, data, evaluation, failure modes and oversight.
  • CC 106Professional Practice and Technical Communication. Turn evidence into clear decisions, limitations, handoffs and explanations.

Four areas of practice.

Specialisation follows the common foundation so learners can connect new tools to concepts they already understand.

01

Applied AI

Problem framing, data, architecture, evaluation and operations.

02

Cybersecurity GRC

Risk, controls, evidence, assurance, incidents and metrics.

03

AI Governance

Inventory, impact, procurement, monitoring, change and retirement.

04

Cloud

Architecture, identity, workload security, resilience and cost.
