Curriculum

Understand the system. Build the evidence.

The curriculum starts with the technical foundations shared by modern technology work, then moves into Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance or Cloud.

The common core.

Every learner begins with the same working language for systems, data, networks, security, AI and technical communication.

The sequence is designed to make later specialization more useful because learners can see how one technology decision affects another.

01SystemsTrace actors, boundaries, dependencies and failure points before selecting a tool.
02Data & privacyExamine source quality, provenance, classification, purpose and responsible handling.
03Networks & cloudBuild a working picture of identity, services, networks and cloud boundaries.
04Security & controlsConnect assets, threats, risk, controls, testing and evidence.
05AI systemsEvaluate models, outputs, failure modes, oversight and change.
06Technical communicationTurn evidence into a decision someone else can understand, challenge and use.

Then go deeper.

Applied AI focuses on problem framing and evaluation. Cybersecurity GRC turns controls into evidence. AI Governance focuses on accountable adoption and oversight. Cloud builds working knowledge of architecture, identity, security and resilience.
