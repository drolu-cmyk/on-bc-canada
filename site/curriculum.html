Curriculum

Systems first. Tools second.

Six common modules establish the technical foundation before learners move deeper into Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud.

  • CC 101Systems Thinking for Digital Work. Map actors, boundaries, incentives, constraints, decisions and failure points.
  • CC 102Data, Evidence and Privacy. Work with data quality, provenance, classification, purpose and retention.
  • CC 103Computing, Networks and Cloud Foundations. Build a working model of computation, networks, identity, services and cloud boundaries.
  • CC 104Security, Risk and Control Thinking. Connect assets, threats, risk, controls, assurance and evidence.
  • CC 105AI Systems Foundations. Understand models, data, evaluation, failure modes and oversight.
  • CC 106Professional Practice and Technical Communication. Turn evidence into clear decisions, limitations, handoffs and explanations.

Then specialize.

The common foundation leads into Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud. The objective is not to memorize a vendor interface. It is to understand enough of the system to make sound decisions when the interface changes.
