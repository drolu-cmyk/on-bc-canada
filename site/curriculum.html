Start with the system. Then use the tool.

Six common modules establish the foundation before learners move into Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud.

Common core.

The sequence builds from systems and data to security, AI and professional communication.

CC 101

Systems Thinking for Digital Work

Map actors, boundaries, incentives, constraints, decisions and failure points.

CC 102

Data, Evidence and Privacy

Understand data quality, provenance, classification, purpose and retention.

CC 103

Computing, Networks and Cloud Foundations

Build a working model of computation, networks, identity, services and cloud boundaries.

CC 104

Security, Risk and Control Thinking

Connect assets, threats, risk, controls, assurance and evidence.

CC 105

AI Systems Foundations

Understand models, data, evaluation, failure modes and oversight.

CC 106

Professional Practice and Technical Communication

Turn evidence into clear decisions, limitations, handoffs and explanations.

Four areas of practice.

Specialisation follows the common foundation so learners can connect new tools to concepts they already understand.

01

Applied AI

Problem framing, data, architecture, evaluation and operations.

02

Cybersecurity GRC

Risk, controls, evidence, assurance, incidents and metrics.

03

AI Governance

Inventory, impact, procurement, monitoring, change and retirement.

04

Cloud

Architecture, identity, workload security, resilience and cost.
