Impact

Evidence before applause.

We want the work to be useful, measurable and credible. That means publishing verified outcomes as they are established rather than filling a page with numbers that cannot be defended.

What we measure.

Impact is more than attendance. We look for evidence that people can understand a system, apply the learning, explain their reasoning and use that capability in a real setting.

  • ParticipationWho is reached, how people participate and whether access is working across different locations and devices.
  • Learning evidenceCompleted exercises, applied work and explanations that show what a learner can actually do.
  • ApplicationHow learners connect Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud concepts to practical decisions.
  • Partnership valueWhether a collaboration produces useful outcomes for the people and organizations involved.

What we will publish.

Verified measures, selected project evidence and documented stories will be added as the record grows. We will distinguish observed results from goals, estimates and future plans.
