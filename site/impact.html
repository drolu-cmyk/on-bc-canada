Impact

Measure the work. Then make the claim.

We do not publish numbers because a page looks stronger with them. Outcomes belong here when the evidence can support them.

What counts.

The useful question is not simply who attended. It is what a person can understand, apply and explain after the learning.

  • ReachParticipation across Ontario, British Columbia and other Canadian locations, including how people access the virtual program.
  • LearningExercises and applied work that show what a learner can actually do, not only what they completed.
  • ApplicationHow Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud concepts are used in practical decisions.
  • PartnershipsWhether a collaboration produces a useful result for the people and organizations involved.

What gets published.

Verified measures, selected project evidence and documented outcomes. Goals, estimates and unsupported claims stay out of the public record.
