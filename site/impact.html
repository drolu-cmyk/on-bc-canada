Impact & evidence

Impact is a record, not a slogan.

We separate verified outcomes from ambition. As programs grow, this page will publish the evidence behind participation, completion, applied work and partner results.

  • ParticipationWho joined, from where and through which program, using counts supported by program records.
  • Learning evidenceWhat learners completed, built or explained, with clear criteria behind any reported result.
  • Transfer to practiceEvidence that learning was applied in work, study or a community setting where that evidence is available.
  • Partnership valueWhat a collaboration produced, what changed and what remains unresolved.

What we will not do.

We will not turn targets into achievements, publish unattributed testimonials as evidence or use numbers that cannot be traced to a record. Credibility is more useful than inflated reach.
