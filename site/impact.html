Impact & evidence

Evidence before claims.

We measure what participation produced, not just how many people passed through a program. Published results are tied to records that can support the claim.

What we look for.

A useful learning program leaves evidence behind: work completed, decisions explained, skills applied and partnerships that produced something concrete.

Those signals matter more than inflated reach numbers without context.

  • ParticipationConfirmed enrolment, geography and program participation drawn from program records.
  • Learning evidenceWork that shows what a learner can build, assess, explain or defend against clear criteria.
  • Applied useEvidence that learning moved into work, study or another practical setting when that evidence is available.
  • Partnership outcomesOutputs, decisions or changes connected to a defined collaboration with a Canadian organization.
