About

Applied learning for work that now crosses AI, security and cloud.

SozoRock Canada develops practical learning and partnership initiatives for people and organizations working through technology change. Our Canadian operations are anchored in Ontario and British Columbia, with virtual participation available nationally.

One foundation. Four areas of practice.

The program connects technical understanding with the decisions people are already making at work. Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud are taught as related disciplines rather than separate technology trends.

  • Applied AIFrame problems, work with data, evaluate outputs and understand how AI systems behave in practice.
  • Cybersecurity GRCConnect risk, controls, evidence, incidents and assurance to real organizational decisions.
  • AI GovernanceAssess how AI systems are introduced, monitored, changed and retired with clear accountability.
  • CloudBuild working knowledge of architecture, identity, workload security, resilience and cost.

How the learning works.

Understand. Start with the system, the language and the boundaries behind the task.

Build. Apply the ideas through guided exercises, cases and technical work.

Explain. Show the evidence, limitations and reasoning behind the result.
