SozoRock Canada

Building capability that travels with people.

Technology changes quickly. Durable capability comes from understanding systems, making sound decisions and being able to explain the evidence behind the work.

Practical by design.

SozoRock Canada develops learning and partnership initiatives that help people and organizations use technology with greater confidence, judgement and accountability. Our work centres on Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud because those areas increasingly meet in the same workplace decisions.

We favour clear concepts, guided practice and visible evidence over tool-specific training that becomes obsolete when platforms change.

What guides the work.

Access matters, but access alone is not enough. Learning should be useful in practice, responsible in its use of data and technology, and understandable to the people who rely on the result.

  • UsefulLearning starts with real decisions, systems and constraints rather than software demonstrations.
  • ResponsibleSecurity, privacy, governance and evidence are built into the work instead of added at the end.
  • TransferableLearners build concepts and judgement that remain valuable as tools, vendors and models change.
  • CanadianThe program is designed for participation across Canada while SozoRock Tech Inc Canada operates from Ontario.
