About SozoRock Canada

Technology changes. Sound judgement travels.

We bring Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud together because real technology decisions rarely stay inside one discipline. Our Canadian work is anchored in Ontario and British Columbia, with virtual participation across the country.

Built for the decisions behind the tools.

Learning starts with systems, evidence and clear technical communication. From there, people build the depth they need in AI, cybersecurity governance, AI governance or cloud.

The result is practical: understand what is happening, make a defensible decision and explain the reasoning to someone else.

Where we work in Canada

Leadership

Dr. Oluwabiyi Adeyemo

Director, Technology Strategy & Applied Learning

Dr. Oluwabiyi Adeyemo works across technology strategy, applied AI, cybersecurity governance and responsible technology adoption. His approach to learning is shaped by work that people can examine, build, defend and improve.

His current and prior work includes supporting university capstone and experiential learning by designing project tasks, supervising applied work and evaluating evidence produced by master’s-level cybersecurity students and undergraduate capstone teams in the United States and Alberta, Canada.

That experience informs SozoRock Canada’s emphasis on practical work, clear evidence and professional judgement rather than tool familiarity alone.
