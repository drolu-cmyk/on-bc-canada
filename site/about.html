About SozoRock Canada

Practical technology capability for Canada.

SozoRock Canada develops applied learning and partnership work in Applied AI, Cybersecurity GRC, AI Governance and Cloud. Ontario and British Columbia are our current focus provinces, with virtual participation available across Canada.

Why these four areas sit together.

Modern technology decisions cross boundaries. An AI project can become a data, security, governance and cloud decision at the same time. Our programs are designed around that reality.

Learners build enough technical depth to understand the system, enough judgement to challenge the result and enough evidence to explain the decision.

Leadership

Dr. Oluwabiyi Adeyemo

Director, Technology Strategy & Innovation

Dr. Oluwabiyi Adeyemo works at the intersection of technology strategy, applied AI, cybersecurity governance and responsible adoption.

His university-linked work has included designing capstone tasks, supervising experiential projects and evaluating evidence produced by master’s-level cybersecurity students and undergraduate capstone teams in the United States and Alberta, Canada.

That work informs a simple standard for SozoRock Canada: learners need more than exposure to tools. They need to make a decision, produce evidence and explain why the work can be trusted.
