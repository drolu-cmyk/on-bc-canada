Four domains. One technical foundation.

The curriculum begins with systems, data, security and technical communication, then moves into four areas of practice.

01

Applied AI

Frame problems, work with data, evaluate outputs and understand how AI systems behave in practice.

02

Cybersecurity GRC

Connect risk, controls, evidence, incidents and assurance to real organisational decisions.

03

AI Governance

Understand how AI systems are assessed, introduced, monitored, changed and retired.

04

Cloud

Build working knowledge of architecture, identity, security, resilience and cost.

Understand. Build. Explain.

Learning is practical, but the goal is not tool familiarity alone. Learners are expected to understand the system, produce evidence and explain their decisions clearly.

Understand

Start with the concepts, language and system boundaries behind the task.

Build

Apply the ideas through guided exercises, cases and technical work.

Explain

Show the evidence, limitations and reasoning behind the result.
