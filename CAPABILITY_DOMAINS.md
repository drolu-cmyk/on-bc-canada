# Capability domains

Applied AI Training for Canada uses 19 capability domains to connect learning, delivery, automation, quality, safety, privacy, funding, and public communication.

| # | Capability domain | Public function |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Control plane | Versioned events, idempotency, retries, and accountable exception routing |
| 2 | Curriculum compiler | Deterministic generation of learner, instructor, lab, rubric, and evidence outputs |
| 3 | Common Core | Six-module foundation and transferable evidence sequence |
| 4 | Applied AI | Problem framing, data, architecture, evaluation, oversight, and operations |
| 5 | Cybersecurity GRC | Risk, controls, assurance, incidents, and metrics |
| 6 | AI Governance | Inventory, impact, lifecycle, procurement, monitoring, and retirement |
| 7 | Cloud | Architecture, identity, workload security, resilience, cost, and assurance |
| 8 | Assessment | Rubrics, moderation, appeals, resubmission, authenticity, and feedback |
| 9 | Learner operations | Intake, consent, capacity, onboarding, withdrawal, and support |
| 10 | Instructor experience | Generated task view, run-of-show, risk flags, and continuity |
| 11 | Virtual delivery | Schedules, attendance, captions, alternatives, and office hours |
| 12 | Safe labs | AWS provisioning, least privilege, spend controls, reset, and incidents |
| 13 | Provider adapters | Model, workflow, storage, collaboration, communication, and credential contracts |
| 14 | Data and privacy | Learner records, data classes, region, retention, deletion, and access |
| 15 | Credentials | Attendance record, verification, corrections, portability, and status |
| 16 | Accessibility and UX | Web, forms, documents, virtual classroom, and alternate formats |
| 17 | Quality review | Evidence room, complaints, governance, audit, corrective action, and improvement |
| 18 | Funding evidence | Theory of change, metrics, cost model, reports, and data room |
| 19 | Public communication | Website, search metadata, claims, analytics, links, and publication acceptance |

The domains form one operating system. Versioned contracts connect the learner journey to instructional evidence, safe infrastructure, accountable decisions, quality records, and funder reporting.
