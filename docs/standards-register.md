# Standards and regulatory reference register

This register identifies the external references that inform program design, evidence, accessibility, security, AI governance, credentials, and jurisdiction review. A reference does not represent certification, accreditation, endorsement, or authorization.

| Reference | Current anchor | Design and evidence focus | Review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Educational organization quality | [ISO 21001:2025](https://www.iso.org/standard/21001) | learner focus, inclusive delivery, quality management, continual improvement, and distance learning | annual quality review or offer change |
| Distance education review | [DEAC Accreditation Handbook](https://www.deac.org/seeking-accreditation/the-deac-accreditation-handbook/) | institutional evidence, learner experience, faculty/administration, review, and reporting discipline | external-review gap assessment |
| Digital accessibility | [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) and [ISO/IEC 40500:2025 notice](https://www.w3.org/press-releases/2025/wcag22-iso-pas/) | public pages, forms, documents, virtual learning materials, and alternate paths | every release and platform change |
| Information security | [ISO/IEC 27001:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) | security management, risk treatment, access, evidence, and improvement | provider or data-flow change |
| AI management | [ISO/IEC 42001:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) | AI policy, lifecycle governance, traceability, monitoring, and improvement | model/provider or high-risk use change |
| AI risk | [ISO/IEC 23894:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html) and [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | AI-specific risk identification, treatment, evaluation, and lifecycle practice | new AI task or incident |
| Cybersecurity | [NIST CSF 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) | cybersecurity outcomes, governance, supply chain, and risk communication | system or threat change |
| Portable credentials | [Open Badges 3.0](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/open-badges) and [CLR Standard 2.0](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/clr) | verifiable, learner-controlled, machine-readable records and portability | credential design change |
| Ontario | [Career-college compliance notices](https://www.ontario.ca/page/career-college-compliance-notices) and [third-party-funded program directive](https://www.ontario.ca/page/policy-directive-exemption-vocational-programs-funded-third-party) | registration, approval, exemption, advertising, and learner-protection questions | tuition, duration, credential, or delivery change |
| British Columbia | [PTIRU regulatory standards](https://www.privatetraininginstitutions.gov.bc.ca/quality-standards) | program thresholds, approval, publication, records, complaints, and learner-protection questions | BC learner volume, tuition, duration, or program change |

## Evidence status vocabulary

- `reference_only`: the reference informs design; no conformance claim exists.
- `mapped`: program controls and evidence map to the reference.
- `tested`: mapped controls have been exercised and results retained.
- `externally_reviewed`: an external reviewer has reviewed the evidence.
- `certified_or_accredited`: an official decision has been issued and its scope is documented.
